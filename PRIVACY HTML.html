Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 29, 2026  ·  Last updated: May 29, 2026

ONJJEM ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the ONJJEM app ("the App"). Please read it carefully.

1. Information We Collect

The App processes the following types of data solely to provide its core functionality:

  • Photos — Images you select or capture within the App for editing and restoration.
  • Face data — Facial features detected within your photos to enable AI-powered restoration and enhancement.

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, or any other personal information.

2. How We Use Your Data

Photos and face data are used exclusively to:

  • Process and restore images within the App
  • Apply AI-based enhancements you request

We do not use your photos or face data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond delivering the App's core features.

3. Data Storage and Retention

Photos and face data are processed on-device or via secure, encrypted transmission to our processing servers. We do not permanently store your photos or face data on our servers. Once processing is complete, data is deleted from our servers. Your original photos remain solely on your device.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your photos or face data with third parties, advertisers, or data brokers. Data may be processed by trusted infrastructure providers (such as cloud computing services) solely to perform the restoration features you request. These providers are contractually bound to handle your data securely and only as directed by us.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with data, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

6. Your Rights

Because we do not retain your photos or face data after processing, there is generally no stored data to access, correct, or delete. If you have questions about data that may have been retained, please contact us at the address below and we will respond promptly.

7. Security

We use industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect data during transmission and processing. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
